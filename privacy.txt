# Privacy Policy for Fabric Composition Inspector

Last Updated: October 2026

The Fabric Composition Inspector Extention is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed and how it is handled.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
The Extension does not collect, record, transmit, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Page Content:** When you visit supported clothing retail websites (such as Amazon, Target, Macy's, Walmart, etc.), the extension parses on-page product specifications and structured metadata (JSON-LD) locally within your browser to detect garment fabric percentages. This analysis happens entirely on your device.
- **No Browsing History Stored:** The extension does not record, log, or store your browsing history or visited URLs.
- **No External Servers:** No scraped data, search inputs, or page contents are sent to external servers or third parties.

### 2. Permissions Justification
- **Host Permissions:** Required strictly to inject the local content script into supported retail domains so it can detect fabric text on product detail pages.
- **Storage:** If enabled, `chrome.storage.local` is used solely to store local client-side preferences (such as minimizing the fabric breakdown card) on your machine.

### 3. Third-Party Sharing and Sale of Data
We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use user data for advertising, creditworthiness evaluation, or market profiling.

### 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy if new features are added. Any changes will be posted to this page.
